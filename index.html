Play fair

A few rules.
A lot more tennis.

These courts are shared by everyone. A little courtesy keeps them moving.

Please use the courts as intended Pickleball should not be played on the tennis courts. During a CHS match, game, or practice, please walk around—not through—the tennis courts to reach the pickleball or basketball courts.
01

Two hours per booking

Each reservation can be 30 minutes to 2 hours long.

02

Two active bookings per day

You may hold up to 2 active bookings on the same calendar day. A canceled or completed booking frees that slot.

03

Cancel when plans change

Can’t make it? Please cancel your booking so another player can use the court.

04

Plan up to 6 months ahead

Public reservations stay within a practical, configurable booking window.

05

Check the conditions

Weather guidance is advisory. Do not use wet, icy, damaged, or otherwise unsafe courts.
